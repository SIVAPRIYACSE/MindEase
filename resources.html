📚 Wellness Resources

Simple activities to support your everyday wellbeing.

🧘

Deep Breathing

Sit comfortably and slowly breathe in through your nose. Hold for a few seconds and gently breathe out.

🌿

Mindfulness

Focus your attention on the present moment. Notice your surroundings without judging them.

🎵

Relaxing Music

Listening to calm music can create a peaceful environment for relaxation.

🚶

Light Exercise

Take a short walk, stretch your body or spend a few minutes moving comfortably.
